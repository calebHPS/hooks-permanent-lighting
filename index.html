Frequently Asked Questions

Still have questions? Take a look at the FAQ or send us a quote request anytime.

What is permanent lighting?

Permanent lighting is a low-profile track mounted along your roofline with programmable LED lights. You control colors, brightness, and schedules from your phone, so there’s no hanging or taking down lights each season.

How do I get started?

Tap Get A Quote and tell us about your home. We’ll follow up with a free quote and plan the layout for your roofline.

Can I change the colors for different holidays?

Yes. You can switch colors, patterns, and brightness any time from your phone, and set schedules for holidays, game days, or everyday accent lighting.

Do you install on businesses too?

Yes. We install permanent lighting for both homes and commercial properties in Lancaster, PA.

What’s your pricing model?

Pricing depends on the length of your roofline and the layout of your lighting. Every quote is free, with no obligation.
