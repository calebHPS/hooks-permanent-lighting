About Us

Locally Owned, Carefully Installed

Hooks Permanent Lighting is a locally owned company that designs and installs permanent roofline lighting for homes and businesses in Lancaster, PA. We’re an independently owned partner company of Hooks Property Services, the window cleaning and Christmas lighting company serving Lancaster.

We started Hooks Permanent Lighting because we saw how much homeowners love great lighting — and how much they dislike hanging it every year. Permanent lighting solves that, and we want every install to look clean, work reliably, and be easy to use.

Our Values

Our work is guided by a few simple values. We believe honesty builds trust, hard work earns respect, and taking ownership leads to better results. These values shape how we quote, install, and support every customer.

  • Integrity in communication and transparent pricing
  • Personal responsibility for every job we take on
  • Strong work ethic with no shortcuts
  • Respect for every home, property, and customer

Part of the Hooks Family

Hooks Permanent Lighting and Hooks Property Services are independently owned partner companies. If you need window cleaning or seasonal Christmas light installation, Hooks Property Services has you covered — and if you want lighting that stays up all year, that’s us.
